A few things to arrange for a smoother return home

A few sensible reminders ♡

  • Ask your hospital what it provides before buying or packing large quantities of diapers, pads, gowns or baby-care products.
  • Carry only medicines prescribed for the mother, preferably in their original packaging, and show them to the care team.
  • Avoid packing unnecessary valuables, strong fragrances, baby powder or products the hospital has not approved.
  • Keep the baby's sleep space at home simple and follow your care team's safe-sleep guidance; don't add pillows or soft toys to the sleep area.
  • Keep the documents folder, phone and one change of clothes easy to access rather than buried at the bottom of the bag.

This is a general planning checklist, not a substitute for your obstetrician's advice or your hospital's admission instructions.
